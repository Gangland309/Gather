Gather v0.9.11

Web post videos start muted on mouse hover and pause when the pointer leaves. Starting a video through hover, autoplay, or manual controls pauses other videos on the current page. Delayed play completion cannot continue after pointer leave. Manual controls remain available when a browser blocks autoplay. Touch devices retain tap controls and Sparks playback.

Includes all v0.9.10 layout, black theme, clickable links and persistent-login changes. No backend changes.
