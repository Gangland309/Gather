# Marketplace posts · 0.8.5

Includes all previous updates. Shop → Post selling / looking for opens a simple Selling, Looking for or Trading composer. Add a title, description, up to six photos, one processed video and a GC asking price or optional wanted-post budget. Selling posts require a positive whole-credit price. Trading posts have no price. Looking-for posts can leave the budget blank and have no available stock.

Verified members can submit personal marketplace posts without creating a full store profile. A personal marketplace profile is created if they do not have one. Existing custom store details are preserved. Posts go to admin review; they become public in the shop after approval. Shop → My listings shows submission status and archive/resubmit controls. The existing full seller/store composer stays there for merchandise, third-party and supplier listings.

Buyers shows Looking-for posts. Sellers shows member selling posts. Trading shows exchange proposals. Shopping retains online-store and supplier listings. Each approved listing has a Message listing owner / Message · I have this button, opening Gather's normal message flow. Incoming message requests still require approval when applicable.

Photo/video ownership and processing checks remain enforced. Blocked users cannot browse each other's listings. Looking-for and trading posts cannot be added to purchase bags. Checkout, credit payment and completed trade workflows remain unavailable; this update enables listing and messaging, not payment processing or supplier fulfillment.

Follow INSTALL-THIS-UPDATE.md and preserve your database and uploads. Mux configuration is needed for videos. No deployment or physical-device/provider testing was performed here.
