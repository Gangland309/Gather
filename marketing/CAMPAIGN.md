## Posting the assets

1. Install this update and sign in with the configured admin account.
2. Open Admin panel → Official Gather page marketing composer.
3. For a picture post, attach one PNG and paste its caption. Publish intentionally; the result is public.
4. For Sparks, remove the picture, upload `gather-launch-spark.mp4`, check processing until ready, add the Spark caption, then publish. Mux must be configured. The official public video post also appears in Sparks.
5. No posts were published automatically by this update. Adjust launch timing to your actual deployment/testing progress. Do not promote gift purchases or background calling as available yet.

## Visual generation

The two campaign concepts were created with the built-in image-generation skill using the existing connected Gather wordmark as a visual reference. Purple/lime branding, realistic adult interest groups, sharp typography, no competitor logos, no fake testimonials, user counts or earnings claims.

Poster one prompt: portrait social campaign; reference connected Gather wordmark; exact headline “Find your people.” and “Gather your world.”; footer “gatherourworld.com”; premium near-black purple/lime photographic interest-group panels; gaming, fishing, beauty, gardening.

Poster two prompt: portrait community campaign; reference connected Gather wordmark; exact text “Same interests.”, “New connections.”, “Gaming. Fishing. Beauty. Gardening.”, “Find your Gathering.”, “gatherourworld.com”; premium purple/lime four photographic interest-group panels.
